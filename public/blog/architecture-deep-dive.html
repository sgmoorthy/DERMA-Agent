Architecture deep dive

How DermaMind.ai fits together

The project is easiest to understand as five cooperating layers: perception, graph grounding, safe execution, statistical validation, and presentation.

Perception

The perception layer abstracts away slide details and produces stable slide-level outputs for downstream use.

Knowledge grounding

The knowledge fabric prevents free-form biological drift by anchoring candidate hypotheses in known entities and relations.

Safe execution

Because the system can generate code dynamically, AST checks, critic logic, and restricted execution are part of the architecture rather than optional extras.

Statistical validation

Survival methods translate a candidate biological claim into a measurable result. In the current codebase, those results are now tracked with both raw p-values and FDR-adjusted q-values.

Presentation

Streamlit dashboards and the Vite landing page expose the workflow for both researchers and external readers.
