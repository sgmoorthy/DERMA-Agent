Research math and safety

Why statistical correction and sandboxing both matter

DermaMind.ai combines classical survival analysis with explicit execution controls so that generated findings are both quantitatively interpretable and operationally auditable.

Survival-analysis backbone

Kaplan–Meier curves, log-rank tests, and Cox models provide clinically legible outputs for candidate findings.

Multiple-testing control

Agentic exploration can generate many hypotheses. The current engine therefore applies session-wide Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction so that exploratory results are reported more honestly.

Attention-style slide pooling

The perception layer now exposes an attention-style pooled slide representation, mirroring the paper’s AMIL-style abstraction even in mock mode.

Execution safety

AST validation, critic checks, restricted imports, and sandboxed execution make it easier to inspect how an analysis was produced and reduce the risk of unsafe generated code.

Takeaway

Scientific usefulness depends on both math and mechanism. A result is more believable when the system can show corrected significance metrics and a constrained execution path.
